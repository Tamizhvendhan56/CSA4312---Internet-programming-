A little about me

Hello, fellow explorer!

I'm Alex, a curious traveller, storyteller and photographer who believes the best journeys are measured in experiences, not miles.

Wanderly began as a small travel diary and grew into a space where I share honest travel stories, useful guides, local discoveries and lessons learned on the road.

My goal is simple: inspire you to explore thoughtfully, connect with people and make memories that last a lifetime.
